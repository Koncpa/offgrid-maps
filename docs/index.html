OffGrid Maps

Standalone offline hiking maps, elevation contours, survival POIs, and local GPX track recording for Wear OS watches.

Privacy Policy Support: appmanufacture.dev@gmail.com

How to Send a GPX Route to Your Watch

  1. Export `.gpx` on your phone (Mapy.com, Komoot, Strava, or Garmin Connect).
  2. Send it to the watch via Quick Share, Bluetooth, or a Wear OS file manager (saved into Download/).
  3. Open it in OffGrid Maps directly from the notification, or open Routes (auto-syncs Download/ & Download/OffGridMaps/) or tap + Import GPX.
  4. Via computer (ADB): run adb push route.gpx /sdcard/Download/OffGridMaps/ and open Routes.
