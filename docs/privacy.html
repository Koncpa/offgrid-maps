Privacy Policy

OffGrid Maps · Standalone Wear OS Hiking Navigation

Package: cz.offgrid.maps · Developer: AppManufacture · Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Offline-First · Zero Telemetry OffGrid Maps is a standalone Wear OS app. It requires no account, contains no ads or analytics SDKs, and never uploads your GPS location or recorded GPX tracks to developer servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

OffGrid Maps does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal identifiers such as your name, email address, phone number, advertising ID, crash telemetry, or usage analytics. There are no user accounts and no third-party advertising or analytics libraries inside the app.

2. Location Data & Background Tracking

OffGrid Maps requests location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and optionally ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION with FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION) solely for core outdoor navigation on your watch:

  • Live map positioning: Displaying your current GPS position on offline hiking maps.
  • GPX track recording: Continuously recording your hiking route while a tracking session is active, including when the watch screen dims or turns off.

Local-only storage: All GPS coordinates and recorded GPX routes stay strictly on your watch in local app storage. The developer never receives or accesses your location history. You can delete recorded tracks in the app or remove all data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling OffGrid Maps.

3. GPX Export & Sharing

When you explicitly choose to export or share a recorded route, the app writes a .gpx file locally or hands it off through Android's system share mechanism (FileProvider) to an app you select. Once shared, that target app's own privacy policy applies.

4. Network Connections & Offline Map Downloads

When you browse online or download offline regional map packages, the watch performs standard HTTPS requests (without user accounts or personal tracking parameters) to fetch public map data:

  • Map Catalog & Region Packages (Cloudflare Workers / R2 CDN, with GitHub Releases fallback): Downloads the public region catalog and compressed hiking trail, POI, and contour ZIP packages you select.
  • Vector Base Map Tiles & Fonts (OpenFreeMap): Streams base vector map tiles and fonts when online or when caching an area on your watch.
  • Elevation Contour Fallback (AWS Open Data Terrain Tiles): Optionally fetches public terrain tiles if elevation contours are not already bundled in your downloaded regional package.
  • On-device Localhost Server (127.0.0.1): Communicates strictly internally on your watch between the local style/tile server and the map renderer; no data leaves the device.

Standard server access metadata (such as IP address and requested file path) may be temporarily processed by those infrastructure providers solely to serve the requested map files.

5. Other Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Shows the ongoing foreground notification while GPS track recording is active.
  • WAKE_LOCK & VIBRATE: Keeps GPS recording reliable during hikes and provides haptic feedback.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Downloads requested offline map packages and checks connectivity beforehand.

6. Children's Privacy

OffGrid Maps does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Policy

If app features or map data sources change, this Privacy Policy will be updated on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy or OffGrid Maps can be sent to appmanufacture.dev@gmail.com.
